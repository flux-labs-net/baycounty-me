What's new

Updates

New pages, new data and corrections, newest first. Subscribe with any feed reader to get them as they happen: RSS feed.

BayCounty.me launched on Oct 20, 2026. The first entries below are the initial pages going live. From here on, each new dataset, data refresh and correction gets its own dated entry.

Change log

  1. Oct 21, 2026An API for BayCounty.me. Every dataset on the site is now available through a free, read-only API with filtering, sorting and CSV output, plus an MCP server so AI assistants can look up and cite these records directly. API
  2. Oct 21, 2026Correction: Money map. The first version of the Money map overstated connections. It counted a donor's contracts again for every official the donor gave to, which roughly doubled the government totals, and it drew lines from officials to governments they don't serve on. The map now puts each donor in the middle: campaign gifts on one side, the governments that pay the donor on the other, each counted once. It marks only gifts to an official whose own government pays that donor, and lists the recorded votes those officials cast on that donor's contracts. The heat map that credited contract dollars to individual officials was removed. Money Map
  3. Oct 21, 2026Who's running in 2026. Every local race on Bay County ballots this year, with each candidate's platform in their own words, links to their campaigns, and their campaign money: what they raised and spent, how much came from individuals, organizations and their own pockets, and every business or committee that gave. Races already decided are shown separately. 2026 Elections
  4. Oct 21, 2026Property tax exemptions, and exemptions on My Address. A new page shows every property tax exemption in the 2026 tax roll, from homestead to disabled veterans, with how many homes get each and how much tax it takes off bills. My Address now lists each exemption on a home by name and uses the certified school and non-school taxable values, which corrects estimates for fully exempt homes and for seniors with the county senior exemption. Exemptions · My Address
  5. Oct 21, 2026School Board votes, and more city votes. How They Voted now includes 104 School Board votes from January 2024 to September 2026 with each member's vote, including budgets, millage and major contracts; all 2024 Panama City Beach council votes, including the FY2025 budget; and Springfield votes from mid-2024 to October 2026, including both final budget hearings. How They Voted
  6. Oct 21, 2026Money map. An interactive map of which businesses and groups gave to which officials, and where those same donors hold contracts with a local government, plus heat maps of giving by government and of contract dollars held by each official's donors. Money Map
  7. Oct 21, 2026Budget explorer. Open any government's FY2026 budget: spending by fund, General Fund spending by department, and revenue by source. Includes Callaway's and Parker's adopted budgets, which weren't on the site before. Budget Explorer
  8. Oct 21, 2026Who gets paid: tourism agencies. Payments by Visit Panama City Beach, Destination Panama City and the Mexico Beach CDC, read from the Clerk of Court's check registers and checked against each month's printed total. Who Gets Paid
  9. Oct 21, 2026Every bond, note and loan. The Debt page now lists each of 61 bond, note and loan issues with the amount borrowed, what is still owed, the rate, the final payment date and what it paid for. Debt
  10. Oct 21, 2026A page for each government. Bay County, the School Board and each of the seven cities now have one page that pulls together who runs it, its budget and property tax rate, debt, audit findings, recorded votes, contracts, projects and upcoming meetings. Governments
  11. Oct 21, 2026Compare governments side by side. Pick any governments and compare budgets, tax rates, debt and staffing, in total or per resident. Compare
  12. Oct 21, 2026Upcoming public meetings. Every board's next meetings, with agenda links and the money items on each agenda, checked daily. Add them all to your phone or computer calendar with one link. Meetings
  13. Oct 21, 2026Campaign money and contracts. The most recent campaign of each current elected official: how much they raised, from individuals and organizations, and which donors also hold contracts with local governments. Individual donors are counted, not named; each official's full report is linked. Campaign Money
  14. Oct 21, 2026Request a public record. Pick a government and a record this site is missing, or describe your own, and get a ready-to-send Florida public records request with the right office's contact details. Records Request
  15. Oct 21, 2026Open data downloads. Every dataset on the site, as CSV and JSON files you can open in a spreadsheet, with a description of each column. Open Data
  16. Oct 21, 2026Zoning and development near your address. My Address now shows the zoning and future land use at the address, development projects and orders within half a mile, recent rezonings, and conditional uses nearby. My Address
  17. Oct 21, 2026BayCounty.me en español. My Address, the records request tool and the glossary are now available in Spanish. Mi dirección, solicitudes de registros y el glosario, en español. En español
  18. Oct 21, 2026Address lookup understands more ways of typing an address. My Address now finds addresses typed with full street types (Drive, Avenue, Boulevard), directions (North, West), highway forms (Hwy 98, US 98, County Road 2321), Saint or St., spelled-out numbers (Tenth Street), MLK, unit numbers, and a city, state and ZIP on the end. If a street type is wrong, it shows the closest matches. My Address
  19. Oct 21, 2026Votes: filters and corrections. Filter recorded votes by government, member, year and outcome, sort by date, amount or closest margin, and share a filtered view by link. Corrections checked against the original minutes: removed two Lynn Haven votes counted twice from a second copy of the same minutes; fixed member votes on a Lynn Haven CRA vote and a Panama City Beach vote that had been misread; restored amounts cut off or misread from scanned pages; and noted fiscal-year and resolution-number typos that appear in the minutes themselves. How They Voted
  20. Oct 21, 2026Clearer vote summaries. Every recorded vote now has a plain-English headline and summary saying what was decided, rewritten from the motion in the minutes. The earlier text was pasted from the minutes and was often hard to read. Each card shows who voted which way and links to the minutes. How They Voted
  21. Oct 21, 2026Correction: flood zones on My Address. Flood zones now come live from FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, which reflects Bay County's October 24, 2024 flood maps. The earlier lookup used an older county copy that missed high-risk zones for some addresses (about 8% in a sample of 300) and showed outdated base flood elevations. Results now also show the FIRM panel and any map revision, with a link to check the address on FEMA's map. Thanks to the readers who reported it. My Address
  22. Oct 21, 2026Link previews. Every page now shows a title, summary and image with its headline number when a link is shared in a text, email or social post. Home
  23. Oct 21, 2026Send a correction from the site. A contact form on the About page sends corrections, records and questions straight to the BayCounty.me team. Email is optional and only used to reply. About
  24. Oct 21, 2026Correction: Lynn Haven commission, and a cleaner officials page. Corey Langford is listed as Lynn Haven's fifth commissioner; Judy Tinder's recorded votes remain on the votes page as a former member. The officials page now shows each board as a simple roster, with notes under names and county officers as cards. Springfield and Parker are described as clerk-run cities with no manager, and Parker's City Clerk, Rachelle Stanley, is listed. Officials · How They Voted
  25. Oct 20, 2026How they voted. Recorded votes on budgets, tax rates, contracts, land and borrowing, with every member's vote and split votes first. Now covers the County Commission and every city: 351 votes, 115 of them split. How They Voted
  26. Oct 20, 2026Contracts and vendors. Major contracts approved by Bay County and its cities since October 2022: winners, how they were awarded, change orders, emergency purchases, the bidding rules, and who the county actually paid the most. Contracts
  27. Oct 20, 2026Fees, utility bills and who runs Bay County. Every flat fee on Bay County tax bills, a typical monthly water, sewer and garbage bill in each city, and a guide to every elected seat, appointed manager and top salary. My Address now shows the actual fees on each parcel. Fees & Bills · Officials · My Address
  28. Oct 20, 2026Designed PDF reports, and a new home page. Export PDF now builds a formatted report on every page, with tables, charts, maps and source links. The home page shows headline numbers and every topic. Home · My Address · CRA Spending · Tax Dollars
  29. Oct 20, 2026Tourist tax and sales tax projects. The 5% bed tax: ten years of collections, how the pennies are split and what Visit Panama City Beach spends. Plus a map of 1,062 half-cent sales tax projects with their status, and the school half-cent. Tourist Tax · Sales Tax Projects
  30. Oct 20, 2026Debt and audit findings. What every Bay County government owes, including big single payments ahead, and three years of audit findings in plain English, from audits filed with the Florida Auditor General. Debt · Audit Findings
  31. Oct 20, 2026Search, and Hurricane Michael recovery money. New: search across every page and record. New page tracking FEMA, HUD and state Hurricane Michael money in Bay County, with all 765 FEMA projects. Search · Hurricane Michael
  32. Oct 20, 2026My Address. Look up any Bay County address for a tax estimate, representatives, schools, flood and evacuation zones, and CRA status. Uses the 2026 property roll. My Address · Home
  33. Oct 20, 2026Amendment 3, and the homestead exemption fix. New Amendment 3 page with the state's estimates for every Bay County government. Calculators now use the inflation-adjusted second homestead exemption ($25,722 for FY2026 bills). Amendment 3 · Tax Dollars · Glossary
  34. Oct 20, 2026Tax dollars and the plain-English guide. How your tax dollars are allocated: bill calculator, six years of rates and levies, and every government's budget. Plus a glossary and tap-to-explain terms across the site. Tax Dollars · Glossary · CRA Spending
  35. Oct 20, 2026CRA Spending launched. Map and ledger of Bay County's six Community Redevelopment Agencies. CRA Spending · About
