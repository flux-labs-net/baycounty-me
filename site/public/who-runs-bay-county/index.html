Who Runs Bay County

Who runs Bay County

Bay County has one county government, seven cities, a school district and five independently elected county officers. Here's who holds each seat, who's appointed to run things day to day, and what top officials are paid.

Data updated Oct 21, 2026
Bay County includes the five county officers
The simple version

Who's in charge of what

  1. Elected boards set policy and budgets. The County Commission, each city commission or council, and the School Board vote on budgets, tax rates and big contracts.
  2. Appointed managers run operations. A county manager or city manager, hired by the board, runs departments and staff. Springfield and Parker have no manager; the board oversees staff directly and a city clerk handles administration.
  3. Five county officers are elected on their own. The Sheriff, Property Appraiser, Tax Collector, Clerk of Court and Supervisor of Elections each run their own office. The County Commission funds most of their budgets. Their pay is set by a state formula.
