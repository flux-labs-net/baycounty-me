What the auditors found

FY2025: 8 findings · FY2024: 11 findings · FY2023: 7 findings
  1. Audit Adjusting Entries (first reported 2007) Repeat

    The auditors had to make significant corrections to the city's books.

  2. Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards Preparation (first reported 2007) Repeat

    The city cannot prepare its own federal grant spending schedule; the auditors help, and multiple year-end grant entries were needed.

  3. Bank Reconciliations (first reported 2007) Repeat

    The pooled cash account in the ledger did not match the monthly and year-end bank reconciliations. Errors or fraud could go undetected.

  4. Account Balances with Subsidiary Ledgers (first reported 2015) Repeat

    Receivables, customer deposits and payables did not agree with their detail ledgers and were not reconciled during the year.
