What the auditors found

8 findings in FY2025 · 26 across 3 audits

Every year outside accountants check the books. "Clean" means the numbers can be trusted overall; "qualified" means the auditor couldn't sign off on part of them. Findings are the to-do list, and a repeat finding means it wasn't fixed.

Springfield · findings by year, oldest to newest: 7 → 11 → 8
  • FY2025 Clean opinion 8 findings Audit report
    1. Audit Adjusting Entries 2025-001 Repeat

      The auditors had to make significant corrections to the city's books.

      Management response: Will try to reduce adjustments; eliminating them would require hiring staff, which management calls cost prohibitive.

    2. Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards Preparation 2025-002 Repeat

      The city cannot prepare its own federal grant spending schedule; the auditors help, and multiple year-end grant entries were needed.

      Management response: Management considers auditor assistance cost-beneficial.

    3. Bank Reconciliations 2025-003 Repeat

      The pooled cash account in the ledger did not match the monthly and year-end bank reconciliations. Errors or fraud could go undetected.

      Management response: Dedicated employee plus management review of statements and a third-person review of entries now in place. Target Sep 30, 2026.

    4. Account Balances with Subsidiary Ledgers 2025-004 Repeat

      Receivables, customer deposits and payables did not agree with their detail ledgers and were not reconciled during the year.

      Management response: Training for finance staff and monthly management review of reconciliations. Target Sep 30, 2026.

    5. Month-end Closing 2025-005 Repeat

      The month-end checklist leaves out major balances, so monthly financial reports given to the Commission are not reliable.

      Management response: Will expand checklist, starting with bank reconciliations and payables. Target Sep 30, 2026.

    6. Activity Classification Review 2025-006 Repeat

      Many revenues and expenses were posted to the wrong accounts or netted together, hiding what was really collected and spent.

      Management response: Monthly financial statement review and staff training. Target Sep 30, 2026.

    7. Segregation of Duties 2025-007 Repeat

      Small staff means one person can approve, record and have custody of assets, including broad accounting system access.

      Management response: Will add mitigating controls; staff must be cross-trained as backups. Target Sep 30, 2026.

    8. Actual Expenditures Exceeded Budget in the General Fund 2025-008

      General Fund spending exceeded the final adopted budget by about $8,069,872 without a budget amendment, violating s. 166.241(3) F.S. The auditor suggested budgeting for debt proceeds and similar sources (the year included the $15 million city complex loan).

      Management response: Will work to stay within budget and amend the budget as statute requires. Target Nov 30, 2026.

Records that would find more

The CRA ledger covers projects and grants, not every payment. These records would show subscriptions, duplicate payments and price differences.

  • Accounts payable check register or vendor payment history for the last three fiscal years, as a CSV or spreadsheet export
    Finds duplicate payments, the same vendor at different prices, and charges that grew with no contract change. Request this record
  • A list of software, license, subscription and telecom contracts, with vendor, annual cost and renewal date
    Finds overlapping subscriptions across departments and between cities. Request this record
  • Year-end CRA fund balances, with the project each balance is committed to
    Shows how much tax increment is sitting idle. Request this record
