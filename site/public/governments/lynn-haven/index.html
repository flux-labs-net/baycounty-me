What the auditors found

1 finding in FY2025 · 3 across 3 audits

Every year outside accountants check the books. "Clean" means the numbers can be trusted overall; "qualified" means the auditor couldn't sign off on part of them. Findings are the to-do list, and a repeat finding means it wasn't fixed.

Lynn Haven · findings by year, oldest to newest: 1 → 1 → 1
  • FY2025 Clean opinion 1 finding Audit report
    1. Reconciliation of Account Balances, Audit Adjustments 2025-001 Repeat

      Auditors had to correct journal entries to finish year-end balances, especially grant revenues and receivables, which also changed the federal/state awards schedule. Auditor cites turnover of key staff and late Hurricane Michael grant reimbursements, but noted significant improvement over the prior year.

      Management response: Hired an Assistant Finance Director in spring 2025, adopted a Finance policies and procedures manual, bought capital-asset software; target Sep 30, 2026.

  • FY2024 Clean opinion 1 finding Audit report
    1. Reconciliation of Account Balances and Audit Adjustments 2024-001 Repeat

      Same issue; classified as a material weakness in FY2024.

      Management response: Substantial progress; downgraded to significant deficiency in FY2025.

  • FY2023 Clean opinion 1 finding Audit report
    1. Reconciliation of Account Balances and Audit Adjustments 2023-001 Repeat

      Same issue; repeat of 2022-001; corrective action not taken.

      Management response: See report.

Records that would find more

The CRA ledger covers projects and grants, not every payment. These records would show subscriptions, duplicate payments and price differences.

  • Accounts payable check register or vendor payment history for the last three fiscal years, as a CSV or spreadsheet export
    Finds duplicate payments, the same vendor at different prices, and charges that grew with no contract change. Request this record
  • A list of software, license, subscription and telecom contracts, with vendor, annual cost and renewal date
    Finds overlapping subscriptions across departments and between cities. Request this record
  • Year-end CRA fund balances, with the project each balance is committed to
    Shows how much tax increment is sitting idle. Request this record
