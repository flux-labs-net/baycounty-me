What the auditors found

7 findings in FY2025 · 21 across 3 audits

Every year outside accountants check the books. "Clean" means the numbers can be trusted overall; "qualified" means the auditor couldn't sign off on part of them. Findings are the to-do list, and a repeat finding means it wasn't fixed.

Parker · findings by year, oldest to newest: 6 → 8 → 7
  • FY2025 Clean opinion 7 findings Audit report
    1. Significant Audit Adjustments 2025-001 Repeat

      The auditors had to propose adjustments before the city's books met accounting standards (GAAP). Material weakness. First reported in 2007.

      Management response: Hired a third-party accountant; expects fewer adjustments now that Hurricane Michael grant work is ending, but not elimination given limited staff. Target Sep 30, 2027.

    2. Lack of Segregation of Duties 2025-002 Repeat

      Too few staff to separate incompatible duties; material weakness reported since 2009.

      Management response: May never be fully resolved given staff size; management does not believe the cost of fixing it would provide substantial benefit. Target Sep 30, 2027.

    3. Reporting Requirements for Grants 2025-003 Repeat

      The audit was not finished within 9 months of year end, so the federal Single Audit package was not filed on time with the Federal Audit Clearinghouse. The city could lose grant funding.

      Management response: Staff turnover delayed reporting; city expects to correct in the coming year.

    4. Insufficient Documentation Supporting Law Enforcement Trust Fund Revenue 2025-004

      The city booked $36,652 from disposed criminal-case evidence as Law Enforcement Trust Fund revenue but could not show a forfeiture judgment or court order proving the money legally belonged to the city. Revenue and fund balance may be overstated by $36,652.

      Management response: See city's corrective action plan in report.

    5. Failure to Timely Transfer Tax Increment Revenues to CRA Trust Fund 2025-005 Repeat

      State law (s. 163.387 F.S.) requires each taxing authority to pay its tax increment to the CRA by January 1. The county paid 4 days late and the city did not pay its own share before year end, leaving the CRA trust fund short about $122,929.

      Management response: Procedures established; the city TIF payment will be built into the budget so it is transferred by January 1. Target Sep 30, 2027.

    6. OPEB Census Errors 2025-006 Repeat

      Employee data sent to the actuary for the retiree-health (OPEB) valuation had multiple errors, producing an inaccurate valuation and delaying the audit.

      Management response: Errors stem from the prior valuation; a new valuation with corrected data and a more detailed census review will be done in FY2026. Target Sep 30, 2027.

    7. Retirement Expense Not Reviewed Timely and Rates Not Updated 2025-007

      The city did not update new state FRS contribution rates effective July 1, 2025 in its payroll system for months, understating retirement expense and liability by about $20,000.

      Management response: Rates updated in FY2026; actual contributions remitted through the state portal were correct; monthly reconciliation and rate-change review will be added. Target Sep 30, 2027.

  • FY2024 Clean opinion 8 findings Audit report
    1. Significant Audit Adjustments 2024-001 Repeat

      Auditor-proposed adjustments needed to meet GAAP.

      Management response: Not corrected; lack of resources.

    2. Lack of Segregation of Duties 2024-002 Repeat

      Too few staff to separate duties.

      Management response: Not corrected; lack of resources.

    3. Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards 2024-003 Repeat

      Weak controls over preparing the financial statements and the federal awards schedule (material weakness); repeat of 2023-003 and 2022-003.

      Management response: Reported corrected in FY2025.

    4. Failure to Timely Transfer Tax Increment Revenues to CRA Trust Fund - City Allocation 2024-004

      For the current year and the two years before, the city did not calculate and transfer its required share of tax increment to its CRA.

      Management response: Partially corrected in FY2025 (past amounts paid but not on time).

    5. Reporting Requirements for Grants 2024-005 Repeat

      Single Audit package not filed on time with the Federal Audit Clearinghouse.

      Management response: Not corrected; staff turnover.

    6. OPEB Census Errors 2024-006

      Inaccurate employee data given to the OPEB actuary.

      Management response: New valuation planned for FY2026.

    7. Lack of Review and Reconciliation of Certain Account Activity 2024-007

      Certain account activity was not consistently reviewed and reconciled.

      Management response: Reported corrected in FY2025.

    8. Reimbursement Requests Not Formally Approved Before Submission (federal, AL 97.036 FEMA Public Assistance) 2024-101

      Hurricane Michael FEMA reimbursement requests were submitted without documented city approval. Significant deficiency in internal control over compliance; no questioned costs.

      Management response: Reported corrected in FY2025.

      Management response: Not expected to be corrected soon due to lack of resources.

      Too few staff to separate duties.
