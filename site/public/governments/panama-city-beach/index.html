Panama City Beach levies no city property tax. Its Front Beach Road CRA receives tax-increment payments, mainly from Bay County (about $22.2M a year, according to the county). The FY2026 budget lists a $22.2M reimbursement from the CRA trust fund. Source

The council approved the budget unanimously. News coverage cited a general budget of about $72 million, with law enforcement as the largest item at nearly $23 million. Source
