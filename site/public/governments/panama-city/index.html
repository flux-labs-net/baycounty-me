What the auditors found

FY2024: 1 finding · FY2023: 2 findings · FY2022: 6 findings
  1. CRA policies, procedures and plan documents (previously 2022-002, 2023-001) Repeat

    The city's Community Redevelopment Agency's policies, procedures and plan documents did not ensure expenditures were proper; assets could be misused or unallowable spending could occur. Reported as a material weakness.
