What the auditors found

1 finding in FY2024 · 13 across 7 audits

Every year outside accountants check the books. "Clean" means the numbers can be trusted overall; "qualified" means the auditor couldn't sign off on part of them. Findings are the to-do list, and a repeat finding means it wasn't fixed.

Panama City · findings by year, oldest to newest: 6 → 2 → 1
  • FY2024 Clean opinion 1 finding Audit report
    1. CRA policies, procedures and plan documents (previously 2022-002, 2023-001) 2024-001 Repeat

      The city's Community Redevelopment Agency's policies, procedures and plan documents did not ensure expenditures were proper; assets could be misused or unallowable spending could occur. Reported as a material weakness.

      Management response: CRA agrees; the city reorganized the CRA and Housing departments and a new Economic Development/CRA Director is revising plans and policies (FY2022 corrective action plan, target Sep 30, 2024, still repeated).

  • FY2023 Clean opinion 2 findings Audit report
    1. CRA policies, procedures and plan documents (previously 2022-002) 2023-001 Repeat

      Same CRA policy deficiency as above.

      Management response: Agrees; see corrective action plan.

    2. Capital asset policies (previously 2022-004) 2023-002 Repeat

      Asset tags were missing on several capital assets and some assets could not be found for testing; procedures did not ensure assets were tagged or properly disposed of.

      Management response: Agrees; hired an Asset and Inventory Control Administrator to re-establish asset accounting.

  • FY2022 Clean opinion 6 findings Audit report
    1. Bank reconciliations (previously 2021-001) 2022-001 Repeat

      Bank accounts were not reconciled on a timely, regular basis, risking misappropriation or misstated records.

      Management response: Hired outside consultant; completed March 14, 2023.

    2. CRA policies, procedures and plan documents 2022-002

      CRA policies did not ensure expenditures were proper.

      Management response: Reorganized CRA and Housing departments; new director; ongoing.

    3. Schedule of Expenditures of Federal Awards and State Financial Assistance (previously 2021-008) 2022-003 Repeat

      Inadequate procedures to prepare the federal/state grant spending schedule, so misstatements might not be caught.

      Management response: Hired an additional accountant and created a Project Management Office for Hurricane Michael rebuilding grants; completed May 31, 2023.

    4. Capital assets 2022-004

      Inadequate capital asset policies (tagging, disposals).

      Management response: Hired Asset and Inventory Control Administrator; target Sep 30, 2024.

    5. Late federal data collection form (previously 2021-011) 2022-005 Repeat

      The Single Audit data collection form was not submitted on time; agencies could withhold, suspend or terminate awards.

      Management response: Added accounting staff and an outside consultant for Hurricane Michael grant accounting; target Sep 30, 2023.

    6. Late reporting (previously 2021-012) 2022-006 Repeat

      Related timeliness finding on required report submission (same corrective action as 2022-005).

      Management response: Same as 2022-005.

Records that would find more

The CRA ledger covers projects and grants, not every payment. These records would show subscriptions, duplicate payments and price differences.

  • Accounts payable check register or vendor payment history for the last three fiscal years, as a CSV or spreadsheet export
    Finds duplicate payments, the same vendor at different prices, and charges that grew with no contract change. Request this record
  • A list of software, license, subscription and telecom contracts, with vendor, annual cost and renewal date
    Finds overlapping subscriptions across departments and between cities. Request this record
  • Year-end CRA fund balances, with the project each balance is committed to
    Shows how much tax increment is sitting idle. Request this record
