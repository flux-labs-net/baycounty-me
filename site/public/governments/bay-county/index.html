What the auditors found

FY2024: 4 findings · FY2023: 2 findings · FY2022: 3 findings
  1. Lack of Documented Review of Required Quarterly Reports (federal, AL 97.036 FEMA Public Assistance)

    3 of 40 quarterly FEMA (Hurricane Michael) reports prepared by the county's consultant did not match approved expenses and support. There was no documented county review before the consultant submitted them. Material weakness; no questioned costs.

  2. Ineligible Expenses Approved for Reimbursement (federal, AL 97.083 SAFER fire staffing grant)

    Two of three reimbursement requests tested included ineligible payroll costs approved by the grant administrator. The federal grantor caught them and paid only eligible costs. Material weakness; no questioned costs.

  3. Material audit adjustments (Bay County financial statements)

    Material adjustments were needed for the financial statements to meet GAAP because county controls did not catch them in time. Material weakness.

  4. Material adjustments - Bay County Property Appraiser

    The Property Appraiser's office needed material adjustments to cash and accounts payable, and a check was improperly dated in its records. Material weakness.
