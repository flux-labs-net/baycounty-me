What the auditors found

4 findings in FY2024 · 9 across 6 audits

Every year outside accountants check the books. "Clean" means the numbers can be trusted overall; "qualified" means the auditor couldn't sign off on part of them. Findings are the to-do list, and a repeat finding means it wasn't fixed.

Bay County · findings by year, oldest to newest: 3 → 2 → 4
  • FY2024 Clean opinion 4 findings Audit report
    1. Lack of Documented Review of Required Quarterly Reports (federal, AL 97.036 FEMA Public Assistance) 2024-001

      3 of 40 quarterly FEMA (Hurricane Michael) reports prepared by the county's consultant did not match approved expenses and support. There was no documented county review before the consultant submitted them. Material weakness; no questioned costs.

      Management response: Will add a formal, signed review of quarterly FEMA reports before submission.

    2. Ineligible Expenses Approved for Reimbursement (federal, AL 97.083 SAFER fire staffing grant) 2024-002

      Two of three reimbursement requests tested included ineligible payroll costs approved by the grant administrator. The federal grantor caught them and paid only eligible costs. Material weakness; no questioned costs.

      Management response: Additional review, including by the Clerk of Court Board Finance Office, documented by email approval.

    3. Material audit adjustments (Bay County financial statements) 2024-003

      Material adjustments were needed for the financial statements to meet GAAP because county controls did not catch them in time. Material weakness.

      Management response: Finance Office will identify and record proposed adjustments more timely, coordinating with departments. Target Oct 1, 2025.

    4. Material adjustments - Bay County Property Appraiser Property Appraiser 2024-001

      The Property Appraiser's office needed material adjustments to cash and accounts payable, and a check was improperly dated in its records. Material weakness.

      Management response: Management agrees.

  • FY2023 Clean opinion 2 findings Audit report
    1. Permit fees not charged per adopted fee schedule Bay County 2023-001 Repeat

      Because of problems in the permit system, the county charged some permit fees that did not match the fee schedule set by Resolutions 3945 and 3957, overcharging in some cases; the county booked a payable for the estimated excess. Auditor recommended fixing the system and remedying affected permits.

      Management response: Corrective action plan attached to report; reported corrected in FY2024.

    2. Material adjustments - Bay County Sheriff Sheriff 2023-001 Repeat

      The Sheriff's office needed material adjustments to cash, expenses, unearned revenue, payables and accrued liabilities. Material weakness.

      Management response: Management agrees.
