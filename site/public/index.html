What's new

All updatesRSS
  1. Oct 21, 2026Clearer vote summaries Every recorded vote now has a plain-English headline and summary saying what was decided, rewritten from the motion in the minutes. The earlier text was pasted from the minutes and was often hard to read. Each card shows who voted which way and links to the minutes.
  2. Oct 21, 2026Correction: flood zones on My Address Flood zones now come live from FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, which reflects Bay County's October 24, 2024 flood maps. The earlier lookup used an older county copy that missed high-risk zones for some addresses (about 8% in a sample of 300) and showed outdated base flood elevations. Results now also show the FIRM panel and any map revision, with a link to check the address on FEMA's map. Thanks to the readers who reported it.
  3. Oct 21, 2026Link previews Every page now shows a title, summary and image with its headline number when a link is shared in a text, email or social post.
  4. Oct 21, 2026Send a correction from the site A contact form on the About page sends corrections, records and questions straight to the BayCounty.me team. Email is optional and only used to reply.

How this site works

Public records only

Figures come from audits, adopted budgets, state data, annual reports and council records. If a number isn't in a record, it isn't on the site.

Sources on every row

Each payment, project or finding links to the document it came from, so you can check it yourself. Download reports as PDF or data as CSV.

Gaps stated plainly

Where a government doesn't publish something, the page says so and names the record to request.
