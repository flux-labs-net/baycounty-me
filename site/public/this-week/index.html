How this is put together

  • Events come from public calendars (Events). Repeating programs and public meetings are left out of this list.
  • New votes and contracts are the ones that first appeared on BayCounty.me last week. Minutes are often approved weeks after a meeting, so a vote can be from an earlier meeting.
  • Votes are ranked to put split votes, failed motions, budgets, taxes and contracts first. Every vote is on How they voted.
