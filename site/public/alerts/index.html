Get involved

Get updates by email

Follow the governments you care about. Once a week, when there's something new, you'll get a short email: new votes and data, corrections, and the meetings coming up.

Follow

We'll email you a link to confirm first. Your address is used only for these updates, never shared or published, and every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Emails come from hello@baycounty.me.

Alerts for one thing

Get an email only when something specific shows up: a big contract, a vote by a board member, a street name on an agenda, or a new campaign report. We check every morning.

We'll email you a link to confirm first. Keyword alerts read agenda item titles where the board posts them as text; PDF-only agendas are matched on the meeting title. A match only means a record mentions what you asked about.

What you'll get

  • Weekly, only when there's news. No email in a quiet week.
  • What's new for each government you follow: votes, contracts, data updates and corrections from the change log.
  • Meetings coming up in the next week, with agenda links when they're posted. See all of them on the meetings page.
  • Prefer a feed reader? Use the RSS feed.

Change or stop your emails

Every email has a Change what you follow link that lets you add or remove governments, turn off alerts or unsubscribe from everything. Lost it? Get a new link by email.
