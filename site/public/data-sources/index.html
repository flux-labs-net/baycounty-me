How we work

  • Public records only. Everything comes from documents anyone can download or request: agendas, minutes, budgets, audits, state databases and official map servers.
  • A link on every figure. Each record carries the link to the document it came from.
  • Nothing made up. Where we estimate (for example future CRA tax increment), the page says so and shows the method. Where a record is missing, we say it is missing.
  • Checked against the source. Totals are added back up and compared with the printed totals; matches between names are checked by hand before anything about a person is published.
  • Privacy. Individual campaign donors are only counted, unless they run a registered business. Pay names only elected officials and department leaders. Home addresses are never shown.
  • Open. Every dataset, including this list, can be downloaded on Open data or read through the API.

How we process each kind of record

Meetings and agendas

Each government's agenda system is checked every 30 minutes on weekdays (when a notice email arrives) and swept twice a day. Every agenda, packet and minutes file is downloaded and fingerprinted; a changed file is kept as a new version. An AI model reads each new agenda into a list of items using only the agenda's own words (it is told not to guess). The meetings list combines the agenda system with the government's calendar; boards with no calendar get dates from their regular schedule, marked "expected".

Checks: Failed fetches are logged and emailed to the site admins. Every version of every file is kept.

20 sources · See the page

Votes

Money votes are read from adopted minutes (or draft minutes printed in the next agenda packet). Each motion is recorded with the roll call by name; the amount comes from the staff memo or resolution attached to the item. Scanned minutes are read with OCR.

Checks: Names are checked against each board's roster. Every split or failed vote found is included.

10 sources · See the page

Contracts and change orders

Awards, change orders and emergency purchases are taken from agenda items and their attached memos and resolutions. An agenda item is a proposal, so where minutes exist the record is checked against them.

Checks: Panama City records were checked against 124 of 126 meetings' minutes and Panama City Beach against 38 of 50; Bay County and Lynn Haven come from agendas only. Known changes are noted on the record.

8 sources · See the page

Budgets

Adopted budget books are downloaded from each government's website and the totals, funds and departments typed into the data. Where a budget book is not posted, the Annual Financial Report the government files with the state is used. Audited revenue and spending come from the state's Local Government Financial Reports.

Checks: Totals are added back up and compared with the printed totals. Each year links to its document.

13 sources · See the page

Audits and debt

Audited financial statements filed with the Florida Auditor General are read for debt (bonds, notes, loans, leases), audit findings and the auditor's opinion.

Checks: Only audited figures are used. When a government's latest audit is not yet filed, the previous year is used and labeled.

3 sources · See the page

Property tax

Tax rates (millage) come from state Department of Revenue and EDR files and the governments' adopted budgets. Assessments and exemptions come from the state's parcel and assessment roll files.

Checks: Rates are checked against each government's adopted millage resolution where posted.

6 sources · See the page

Pay

Pay comes from budget pay plans, published salary schedules, job postings and the state's report on officials' salaries.

Checks: Only elected officials and department leaders are named. Other staff are counted, not named.

12 sources · See the page

Campaign money

Summary totals and the itemized export for each campaign are downloaded from the Supervisor of Elections' campaign finance system every day. Amended and deleted lines are applied, then businesses are matched to state corporate records.

Checks: Itemized sums are checked against the Supervisor's summary totals. Individual donors are only counted, never named, unless they run a registered business.

2 sources · See the page

Elections

Candidate lists, status and official results come from the Supervisor of Elections. Platforms come from candidates' own sites.

Checks: Office names are checked against the Notice of General Election.

2 sources · See the page

Businesses and owners

The state's full corporate file (about 18 GB, updated quarterly) is filtered to businesses with an address in the county. Officers, managers and registered agents are grouped into people and matched to officials and donors.

Checks: A name match is only a lead: nothing about a person is published until it is checked by hand. Street addresses are never stored or shown.

2 sources · See the page

Nonprofits

Every tax-exempt organization with a county address is pulled from IRS data, with every 990, 990-EZ and 990-PF e-filed since 2019 (board members, pay, grants, revenue). State charity registrations and state corporate filings are joined in.

Checks: Organizations are joined by EIN, then by exact name. Official-to-board matches are checked by hand before they are tagged.

4 sources · See the page

Chambers of commerce

Board, staff and program lists are copied by hand from each chamber's website. Upcoming events are read every day from the Panama City Beach Chamber's public calendar feed; the Bay County Chamber's calendar platform turns automated readers away.

Checks: Board members are joined to other local filings by first and last name, and to an official only where the chamber names their government post. Organizations are joined to governments, paid companies, nonprofits or one Sunbiz filing with the exact same name.

3 sources · See the page

Military and the bases

News stories are read every day from the DVIDS feeds of the units at Tyndall Air Force Base and Naval Support Activity Panama City; the base websites turn automated readers away. Federal contracts come from USAspending.gov: every contract action since 2016 for a company with a business address in the county. Base facts and commanders are checked by hand.

Checks: Contractors are joined to a local vendor, or to a business campaign donor through its Sunbiz filing, when the names match; otherwise each gets its own profile. Amounts are money obligated since January 1, 2016.

3 sources · See the page

Redevelopment agencies (CRAs)

CRA spending is read from CRA annual reports, agendas, budgets and audits, one ledger line per payment or project. Tax increment comes from CRA reports, audits and budgets, split between the county and city by millage.

Checks: Each line carries its source and a confidence level; amounts inside a larger total are shown but counted once. Estimates are checked against official county figures.

8 sources · See the page

Hurricane Michael

FEMA Public Assistance projects and applicants for disaster DR-4399 are downloaded from OpenFEMA every week.

Checks: The data is FEMA's own; nothing is retyped.

1 sources · See the page

Sales tax projects

Infrastructure sales tax projects are downloaded from Bay County's map server every week and matched to the published project schedule.

Checks: Matches between the map and the schedule are listed with their method.

3 sources · See the page

Tourist tax

Bed tax collections come from the Clerk's monthly reports. Payments by the three tourism agencies come from the Clerk's monthly check registers, read from text PDFs in the Clerk's public records search. State tax rates and history come from EDR.

Checks: Every agency-month matches the register's printed total to the cent. Payments to private individuals are counted, not listed.

4 sources · See the page

Fees and bills

Assessments come from the state's non-ad valorem assessment roll file. Utility rates and fees come from city and county fee schedules and budget books.

Checks: Where a code-to-name match is inferred rather than published, it is labeled.

8 sources · See the page

My Address lookups

Address lookups go straight from your browser to the county's and FEMA's public map servers at the moment you search.

Checks: Nothing you look up is sent to or stored by BayCounty.me.

4 sources · See the page

Officials by year

Who held each seat since 2016 is built from election results, minutes, budget books, city websites and news reports.

Checks: Each term names its source. Stretches nobody could confirm are marked unconfirmed, not guessed.

1 sources · See the page

Public records contacts

Records custodians, portals and policies are taken from each agency's official pages.

Checks: Only portals linked from an official page are listed.

1 sources · See the page

News and context

Local news reports are cited for context (for example an arrest, a resignation or a policy change) when no official document says it.

Checks: News is never the only source for a dollar figure.
