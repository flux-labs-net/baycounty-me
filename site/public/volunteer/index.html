No nonprofits match.

Volunteer counts are each organization's own estimate on Form 990, Part I, line 6. Statewide groups with a Bay County address (a state VFW department, for example) report for the whole group. Smaller nonprofits file the 990-EZ or 990-N, which don't ask, so many active groups aren't counted here: see every nonprofit.

Getting started

How to help

  1. Pick a cause and a schedule. Ongoing roles like mentoring ask for a few hours a month; one-day events need no long commitment.
  2. Sign up with the organization. Many roles that work with children or patients ask for a background check and a short training first.
  3. No need listed? Most nonprofits below take volunteers year-round. Visit their website or call and ask what they need.

Where this comes from

  • Open needs are read every day from United Way of Northwest Florida's volunteer center and from Idealist's listings for Bay County towns. Only needs in a Bay County town or ZIP are kept. We copy the title, organization, what volunteers do, when, and the city; no addresses or contact names.
  • Organizations are joined to local nonprofits by exact name (a few checked by hand), so their names link to the nonprofit's profile with its filings, board and public money.
  • Causes are plain groupings made from each need's words and the nonprofit's IRS category.
  • Volunteer counts, staff and revenue come from each nonprofit's latest e-filed IRS Form 990.
  • Data: volunteer-needs (CSV) · volunteer-orgs (CSV).
