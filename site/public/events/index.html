Where this comes from

  • Read every morning from each organizer's public calendar feed: the county and city calendars, The Events Calendar feeds on the library, visitor bureau and city sites, and the Mexico Beach calendar export.
  • Only what the calendar publishes: the title, dates, place name, town and a link. Library events outside Bay County are left out.
  • Not here yet: Panama City Beach's city calendar (its feed is empty), Springfield, Visit Panama City Beach and the Bay County Chamber, whose calendars have no public feed we can read.
