What the map does and doesn't show

  1. A gift and a contract are two separate facts. A line to an official is a campaign gift. A line to a government is that government's contracts or payments to the same business. The map never adds contract dollars to an official.
  2. Officials only vote on their own government's contracts. A county commissioner has no say in a city's contracts. The connections that can matter are marked: a gift to an official whose own government pays that donor.
  3. None of this shows wrongdoing. Contracts are usually bid, and most are approved by the full board, often on a consent agenda with routine items. The dates are shown so you can see what came first.

Sources and method

Gifts come from the officials' most recent campaign reports with the Supervisor of Elections. Contracts and payments come from the contracts, change orders and check registers on this site, matched by organization name; each donor's contracts with a government are counted once. Only organizations are shown; gifts from individual people, including a company's owners or employees, are counted on the Campaign Money page but not named or linked to a company.
