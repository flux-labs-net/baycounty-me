Gifts over the $1,000 limit

One organization, one candidate, one election

Florida limits each contributor to $1,000 per election for a local candidate (s. 106.08, F.S.); the primary and the general each count as their own election. These are cases where a campaign's own reports show more than that from one organization, net of refunds. Each was checked by hand. Open means no explanation has been found yet. Resolved means it was explained (for example a reporting error, an amended report, a refund, a check meant for the general election, or an Elections Commission ruling), and the explanation is shown.

About this list

  • This is legal. Under Florida law each corporation or LLC is its own contributor with its own limit ($1,000 per election for local offices), so one owner's companies can each give. Giving in someone else's name is what the law forbids (s. 106.08(5), F.S.). A group here is a pattern in public records, not a finding of wrongdoing.
  • How businesses are linked. Business contributors in the Supervisor of Elections reports are matched to the Florida Division of Corporations (Sunbiz) file. Businesses are grouped when they list the same person as an officer, manager or registered agent, matched by name and address. Corporate officers on 25 or more companies and registered-agent services are left out.
  • Years. Groups are listed by the election the checks were for, newest first. Candidates often raise money a year or more ahead, so checks for a 2024 race can be dated 2023; each card says when its checks were written.
  • Inactive businesses are flagged. A business marked Inactive is dissolved, revoked or no longer filing annual reports with the state; "since" is the last year it filed. Its gift was made while it was still filing.
  • Over the limit. A reported overage is not a finding of wrongdoing. Reports can contain typing errors, a check written before the primary can be meant for the general election, and a campaign fixes an overage by refunding it. Only the Florida Elections Commission decides whether the law was broken. Data: /api/v1/datasets/over-limit-gifts.
  • People aren't named. BayCounty.me doesn't name individual donors. Each business links to its public Sunbiz record.
  • Something wrong? If a business is grouped by mistake, tell us and we'll correct it. The data is also at /api/v1/datasets/shared-owner-groups.
