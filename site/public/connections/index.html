    Sources and method

    Payment records cover contracts, change orders, emergency purchases, check registers, tourism agency payments and CRA ledger records, from agendas, check registers, tourism agency payment files and each redevelopment agency's reports; every record links to its source. CRA ledger items that are only proposed or planned stay on the CRA ledger. Payees are matched to the site's vendor list by name. 598 payees are named; grant programs whose recipients aren't named are grouped, and private home addresses are left out. Campaign gifts come from officials' most recent reports, past campaigns since 2016 and 2026 candidates' reports with the Supervisor of Elections; 49 businesses both gave to a campaign and were paid in these records. Votes come from meeting minutes (dataset donor-votes): a vote is listed when the motion names a company that gave to that member's campaign, or whose owner did. A gift and a payment appearing together is a fact about the records. It says nothing about why either happened, and most spending is approved by the full board.
