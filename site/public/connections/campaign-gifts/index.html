The simple version

Reading campaign money

  1. Candidates must report every contribution. Florida caps gifts to local candidates at $1,000 per person or business per election, and every report is public.
  2. Businesses that work with government often give. It's legal and common. A donor that also has a contract is a fact in the records, not proof that one led to the other.
  3. We name organizations, and people who run a business. Other gifts from individuals are shown only as a count and total. The full reports, with every name, are linked for each official.
