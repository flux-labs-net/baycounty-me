    Where this comes from

    • Providers and rules are copied by hand from each city's and the county's own utility and sanitation pages, rate ordinances and the Florida Public Service Commission's filings. Each row links its source.
    • Your city comes from the county's city limits map. Your pickup day comes live from the city's route map where it publishes one (Panama City, Lynn Haven, Callaway and Parker). Other cities list their days by side of town, or leave it to your hauler.
    • Electric co-op territory comes from a national map of electric service areas. FPL and co-op lines can run through the same neighborhood, so your bill is the sure answer.
    • Internet: providers change by street; the FCC broadband map lists every one at an address.
    • Data: utility-providers (CSV) · trash-pickup (CSV) · trash-holidays (CSV) · utility-rates (CSV) · one address: /api/v1/utilities.
