                      Send the files your own systems already make: the check register, the budget-vs-actual report, contracts, a payroll or position list, board appointments, fee changes. It takes two steps. First you see a report of what the file says (totals by month and department, the biggest payees, the first and last check number) and confirm it’s the right file; nothing is taken in before that. Then we check it against records we collect on our own (approvals in your agendas and minutes, the adopted budget, the state’s yearly figures, published pay). If everything matches it goes on BayCounty.me; if anything doesn’t, it waits for review and you’ll see what didn’t match. The same privacy rules apply as everywhere else: payments to private people are listed without their name, and payroll never names employees. Nothing you send changes numbers already on the site.
