John Haley

30+ years with Bay District Schools, including principal of J.R. Arnold High and district Executive Director of Operations (per campaign site).

Campaign website · facebook.com

In their words
  • Says board decisions should start with whether they benefit students ("Students First, Politics Last").

  • Supports strong school security, school resource officers, practiced emergency protocols and action on bullying.
  • Says classroom technology should support teachers, not replace them.
  • Pledges fiscal responsibility and a stronger connection between the District Office and local schools.

How we did this

Platforms are summarized only from each candidate's own campaign website, campaign social pages or questionnaire answers they wrote, with links to each source. If we couldn't find a statement from the candidate, we say so rather than fill in from news coverage. Candidates are listed alphabetically.

Money comes from the campaign reports candidates file with the Bay County Supervisor of Elections, through the date shown. Organizations are listed by name; gifts from individual people are counted and totaled, and every name is in the linked report. Florida caps gifts to local candidates at $1,000 per person or business per election.

Sitting officials' past campaigns are on the Campaign Money page.

What we couldn't find
  • Campaign Facebook/Instagram pages could not be read (Facebook blocks automated access). Platforms come only from candidate websites and the candidate statements filed with the SOE (shown on each candidate's VoterFocus page).
