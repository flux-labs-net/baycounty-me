Where this comes from

  • The count is read from the Supervisor of Elections' results site about every 20 seconds while votes are counted, and each update is saved, so the trend lines show how every race moved through the night.
  • Statewide and district totals for state and federal races, amendments and judges come from the Florida Division of Elections' county-by-county results file, read once a minute. They can trail the county sites by a few minutes.
  • Unofficial until the county canvassing board certifies the results. Provisional and late overseas ballots can still change close races, and a race within half a point may go to a recount.
  • A vote-type figure shown as "—" is 1 to 29 votes, which Florida law keeps from being reported. City races and questions are voted only by that city's voters.
  • Local candidates link to their profiles, with what each raised from campaign finance reports (2026 races).
  • Data: /api/v1/election-live (the latest count) · /api/v1/election-live/history (every update) · past elections in election-results.
