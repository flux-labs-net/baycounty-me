Local races first

"Most votes" marks the leader once results are final. In a primary or a race with more than two candidates, the leader may still face a runoff or the general election. A count shown as "—" is 1 to 29 votes, which the law keeps from being reported. Candidate names link to their profiles on this site; for campaign money see past local elections and the 2026 races.

Where this comes from

  • Results are read from the Supervisor of Elections' election results site, the same figures the Supervisor certifies. Elections from the last 60 days are read again every day, and every 20 minutes on election night.
  • On a city election day the Supervisor also posts each city's own view of the same contests; those are counted once here. For the 2018 U.S. Senate, Governor and Agriculture Commissioner races, the recount figures replace the first count.
  • Data: elections (CSV) · election-results (CSV).
