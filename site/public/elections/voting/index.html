Election Day

Polls are open 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. Vote only at your own precinct's polling place; bring a photo ID with your signature.

Vote by mail

Your ballot must reach the Elections Office or an early voting drop box by 7 p.m. on Election Day. Precincts don't take mail ballots.

    Where this comes from

    • Dates come from the Supervisor of Elections' voter guide and early voting page; the Supervisor's events calendar is read every day.
    • Polling places are read from the Supervisor's precinct directory; early voting sites are copied from the Supervisor's early voting map before each countywide election. Map points come from Bay County's address points.
    • Races on your ballot are this site's list of 2026 local races still on the ballot (2026 races), matched to your city. School Board and County Commission seats are elected countywide.
    • Data: election-dates (CSV) · voting-locations (CSV) · candidates-2026.
