How we did this

Every figure comes from the campaign reports candidates filed with the Bay County Supervisor of Elections. Raised is money plus in-kind gifts, including any loans from the candidate. Spent includes refunds and leftover money a closed campaign gave away, so it often equals what was raised.

Each campaign's itemized report was checked against the Supervisor of Elections' totals: 293 match exactly, 38 raised nothing, and 11 differ slightly (the Supervisor of Elections' totals are shown). Organizations are named as filed. A filing that coded a person as a business was corrected, so that person is counted with the individuals.

The 2026 races are on Who's running in 2026. Download this data as campaign-history and campaign-contributions-history, or use the API.

What isn't here
  • Left out (party posts and a federal race that file with the SOE): Rep State Committeeman (2016) x2; Rep State Committeeman (2020) x2; Rep State Committeeman (2024) x2; Rep State Committeewoman (2016) x2; Rep State Committeewoman (2020) x3; Rep State Committeewoman (2024) x3; United States Senate (2016).
  • Campaigns whose itemized lines do not add up to the SOE summary are flagged in reconciliation; the SOE summary figures are used for totals.
  • Political committees (for example referendum committees) are not candidates and are not included.
