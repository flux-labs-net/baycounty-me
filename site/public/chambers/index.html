The simple version

What a chamber of commerce is

  1. A private group of businesses. Members pay dues to belong. A chamber is a nonprofit business league (501(c)(6)), not a government agency, and it isn't part of the U.S. or Florida Chamber.
  2. It connects, promotes and speaks up. Chambers run networking events, leadership classes and ribbon cuttings, keep a member directory, and take positions on local issues such as taxes, roads and the bases.
  3. Why it's on this site. Their boards bring together business owners, public agency heads and military leaders, some of their members do business with local governments, and the tourism agencies pay them for events and sponsorships.

The two chambers

Figures from each chamber's latest IRS Form 990

Bay County Chamber of Commerce

Building a Better Bay · Since 1913

An independent, member-owned business group (not a government agency) that runs networking events, leadership programs and advocacy for Bay County businesses. Its foundation runs Leadership Bay, Junior Leadership Bay and Bay Young Professionals.

Serves: Callaway, Lynn Haven, Mexico Beach, Panama City, Panama City Beach, Parker, Springfield and unincorporated Bay County. The board meets at 11 a.m. on the third Thursday of each month; members are welcome. The executive committee meets at 11 a.m. on the second Thursday; those meetings are closed.

Where this comes from

  • Boards, staff and programs are copied from each chamber's own website: names, titles and the organization each board member represents. No phone numbers, emails or addresses.
  • Photos are the headshots each chamber publishes on its own board and staff pages. To have one taken down, use the contact form.
  • Events are read every day from the Panama City Beach Chamber's public calendar feed. The Bay County Chamber's calendar runs on a members platform that turns automated readers away, so its regular programs are listed instead.
  • Revenue, staff and public money come from IRS Form 990 filings and the tourism agencies' payment records, the same data as the Nonprofits page.
  • Member lists are on each chamber's directory; they aren't copied here yet.
  • Data: chambers (CSV) · chamber-leaders (CSV) · chamber-events (CSV).
