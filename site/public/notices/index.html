Every notice, newest first

The date shown with each notice is the first date it mentions after it was published: the meeting, the hearing or when bids are due. Check the notice itself before you go.

Where this comes from

  • Notices are read every day from the county's public notice website, where local governments publish legal notices under Florida law (s. 50.0311, F.S.). The site shows the last 30 days; notices already on file stay here as an archive.
  • Left out: court and private notices on the same site (foreclosures, tax deed applications, probate, notices to creditors, name changes), because they name private people and their homes.
  • Government is matched from the notice's opening words; a notice from an agency that isn't one of the county's local governments (the port, the college, a community development district) is listed under other public agencies.
  • Data: public-notices (CSV).
