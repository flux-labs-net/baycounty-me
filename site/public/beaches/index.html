What the flags mean

The international beach warning flags flown on every public beach here
Double red: Water closed to the publicSwimming and wading are against the law while double red flags fly (fines apply in Panama City Beach and unincorporated Bay County).
Red: High hazardHigh surf and/or strong currents. Experienced swimmers only, and only close to shore.
Yellow: Medium hazardModerate surf and/or currents. Use caution.
Green: Low hazardCalm conditions. Rip currents can still form near piers, jetties and sandbars.
Purple: Dangerous marine lifeJellyfish, stingrays or other marine life seen. Flies with another flag.

Double red flags close the water: going in can bring a fine in Panama City Beach and unincorporated Bay County. No flag flying doesn't mean the water is safe. Flags recorded on 1 day since October 27, 2026: 1 yellow.

Where this comes from

  • Flag and forecast: the National Weather Service Tallahassee surf zone forecast for coastal Bay County, read every morning and again in your browser when you open this page. The forecast lists the flag color the beach officials report to the weather service.
  • Water quality: the Florida Department of Health's Healthy Beaches Program sampling sites and results, read every day. The state's lookup shows each site's latest 50 samples; we keep every sample as it comes in.
  • Beach access and parking: Bay County's and Panama City Beach's public GIS maps and the Florida DEP coastal access survey.
  • Data: beach-conditions · beach-water-sites · beach-water-samples · beach-access · beach-parking (CSV on Open data).
