On the 2026 ballot: City of Panama City Beach Council, Ward 1

on ballot
  • Proposes a Homestead Assessment Discount Program to lower city fire and stormwater assessments for homesteaded residents.
  • Wants free spectator access to the Russell-Fields City Pier for residents, with possible changes to visitor fees to cover the cost.
  • Wants to support police, fire, EMS and lifeguards, continue the current Spring Break approach and strengthen emergency preparedness.
  • Wants roads, water, sewer and stormwater systems to keep pace with growth, with new development paying its fair share.
  • Wants to protect neighborhoods from excessive density, invest in parks and youth recreation, and improve sidewalks.
  • Supports an annual Tourism Local Investment and Impact Report on how tourism dollars benefit residents.
