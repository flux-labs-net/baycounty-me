What is counted. Every recorded vote Richard Wolff cast on a contract award, purchase, task order, change order or renewal since 2016 that names one clear dollar figure. The figure comes from the record of the vote itself (or from a contract record tied to it), never a guess. Yes counts only items that passed; voted no counts items they voted against, whether they passed or not. “Up to” marks a not-to-exceed ceiling and “a year” one year’s price. An item voted on twice is counted once.

Not in the totals, from 154 contract and consent agenda votes on file:

  • 16 consent agendas: many items pass in one vote, and the minutes do not give each item’s cost
  • 60 votes: no dollar figure in the record of the vote
  • 5 votes: more than one figure (a bid plus contingency, a range), or a postponed or unclear motion
  • 38 votes: not new spending: a credit, sale, lease, fee, payment on work already approved, grant received or rate

So the totals are a floor, not everything the board spent while Richard Wolff served. Votes are from the minutes on file (which years we have); a vote is a fact in the record and does not show why anyone voted as they did. Every vote, counted or not, is in the contract-vote-dollars dataset.
