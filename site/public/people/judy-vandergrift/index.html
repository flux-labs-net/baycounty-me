What is counted. Every recorded vote Judy Vandergrift cast on a contract award, purchase, task order, change order or renewal since 2016 that names one clear dollar figure. The figure comes from the record of the vote itself (or from a contract record tied to it), never a guess. Yes counts only items that passed; voted no counts items they voted against, whether they passed or not. “Up to” marks a not-to-exceed ceiling and “a year” one year’s price. An item voted on twice is counted once.

Not in the totals, from 130 contract and consent agenda votes on file:

  • 65 consent agendas: many items pass in one vote, and the minutes do not give each item’s cost
  • 21 votes: no dollar figure in the record of the vote
  • 2 votes: more than one figure (a bid plus contingency, a range), or a postponed or unclear motion
  • 6 votes: not new spending: a credit, sale, lease, fee, payment on work already approved, grant received or rate
  • 1 repeat vote: an item already counted on a later vote
