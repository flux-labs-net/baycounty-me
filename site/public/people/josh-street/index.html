Yes counts only items that passed; voted no counts items they voted against, whether they passed or not. “Up to” marks a not-to-exceed ceiling and “a year” one year’s price. An item voted on twice is counted once.

Not in the totals, from 684 contract and consent agenda votes on file:

  • 121 consent agendas: many items pass in one vote, and the minutes do not give each item’s cost
  • 198 votes: no dollar figure in the record of the vote
  • 31 votes: more than one figure (a bid plus contingency, a range), or a postponed or unclear motion
  • 79 votes: not new spending: a credit, sale, lease, fee, payment on work already approved, grant received or rate
  • 4 repeat votes: an item already counted on a later vote
