What is counted. Every recorded vote Richard Chval cast on a contract award, purchase, task order, change order or renewal since 2016 that names one clear dollar figure. Yes counts only items that passed; voted no counts items they voted against, whether they passed or not. “Up to” marks a not-to-exceed ceiling and “a year” one year’s price. An item voted on twice is counted once.
