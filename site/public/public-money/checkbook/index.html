Where this comes from

  • The government's own check register, exported from its accounting system and sent to BayCounty.me each month. A file is published only after its payments add up to the total printed on the report.
  • Voided checks are left out. A check that was written and then voided would otherwise count twice.
  • Private individuals aren't named. Refunds, reimbursements and other payments to people show as "Individual (name withheld)", counted in every total. Companies, agencies and nonprofits are named and linked to their profiles.
  • Fiscal years start October 1. FY2026 runs from October 2025 to September 2026.
  • Data: /api/v1/checkbook · every payment: checkbook-payments.
