The simple version

Reading campaign money

  1. Candidates must report every contribution. Florida caps gifts to local candidates at $1,000 per person or business per election, and every report is public.
  2. Businesses that work with government often give. It's legal and common. A donor that also has a contract is a fact in the records, not proof that one led to the other.
  3. We name organizations, not people. Gifts from individuals are shown only as a count and total. The full reports, with every name, are linked for each official.

Sources and gaps

Every figure comes from the Bay County Supervisor of Elections' campaign finance system, including the itemized reports, which add up to the official totals. Organization names are shown as filed, except that spelling variants of the same name are combined, and names the filing system cut off at 30 characters are completed where the full name is clear.
