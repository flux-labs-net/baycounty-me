The simple version

How disaster money works

  1. FEMA pays governments back. After a disaster declaration, FEMA's Public Assistance program reimburses cities, the county, schools and some nonprofits for debris cleanup, emergency work and rebuilding public buildings. Each job is a "project" with its own paperwork.
  2. FEMA helps households. Individual Assistance gives grants to homeowners and renters for repairs, temporary rent and other needs.
  3. HUD funds the long rebuild. Congress later sent Florida disaster block grants (CDBG-DR), which the state handed out through "Rebuild Florida" for housing, infrastructure and downtowns.
  4. Loans and insurance fill in. SBA disaster loans and flood insurance claims went straight to residents and businesses.

The Lynn Haven cleanup corruption case

From federal court filings and news coverage

Federal prosecutors charged Lynn Haven officials and contractors over storm cleanup billing after Michael. This is a timeline of what the Justice Department announced and what courts decided.

  1. Oct 16, 2018Lynn Haven adopted a local state of emergency after Hurricane Michael, delegating emergency contracting powers to the mayor or her designee; city entered an emergency agreement with Erosion Control Specialists (ECS) for clean-up (as described in DOJ release). Source
  2. Nov 20, 201935-count indictment announced against former City Manager Michael Edward White, Community Services Director David Wayne Horton, ECS owner David Mitchelle White, Greenleaf Lawn Care owner Joshua Daniel Anderson, and Shannon Delores Rodriguez. DOJ alleged about $5 million in fraudulent invoices; examples included trash-pickup invoices of $332,387.76 and $479,020.68. Source
  3. Aug 19, 2020DOJ announced a 64-count indictment (returned Aug 18) of Mayor Margo Anderson and City Attorney Joseph Adam Albritton; alleged $1.8M billed for unnecessary ECS trash pickup, >$48,000 and $25,000 in private work at officials' homes billed as public work, and a $106,000 motorhome from 'Company B'. The release said all five 2019 defendants had pleaded guilty. Source
  4. Mar 18, 2021Superseding indictment charged developer David James Finch (Phoenix Construction Services) and former Commissioner Antonius Barnes; release says Lynn Haven paid Finch more than $2M in vegetative debris disposal fees. Source
  5. Sep 24, 2021Albritton pleaded guilty to wire fraud and honest services wire fraud; other charges dropped. Source
  6. Oct 7, 2021Barnes pleaded guilty to making a false statement to a federally insured institution; other charges to be dropped. Source
  7. 2023-02Anderson's plea agreement reduced charges to one felony count of lying to the FBI (as reported). Source
  8. 2023-03Finch's first trial ended in a mistrial (as reported). Source
  9. May 8, 2023Sentencing reported: Michael White and David White 42 months in prison each; Josh Anderson and David Horton one year probation including 9 months house arrest; Shannon Rodriguez one year probation. Pleas: M. White 4 counts (conspiracy to commit wire fraud, wire fraud, 2 honest services wire fraud); D. White conspiracy, 2 wire fraud, false claim; Horton wire fraud; J. Anderson wire fraud; Rodriguez conspiracy and wire fraud. Source
  10. Jun 5, 2023Margo Anderson sentenced to one month in prison, one year probation, 100 hours community service. Source
  11. 2023-06Antonius Barnes sentenced (reported later as 45 days in prison). Source
  12. Jul 14, 2023Restitution reported: David White (ECS owner) ordered to pay $4,745,999; Michael White $55,000; David Horton $5,000. Margo Anderson not ordered to pay restitution. Source
  13. Oct 26, 2023Jury found James Finch not guilty on a federal program bribery charge in his retrial (alleged $5,000 check to Barnes, Dec 4, 2017). Source
  14. Feb 8, 2024Albritton, the last defendant, sentenced to one year probation and $31,000 restitution. Reported that every defendant except Finch pleaded guilty to at least one felony. Source
Tyndall Air Force Base is being rebuilt with federal military money, separate from local government recovery. Air Force cost estimates have ranged from $2.7B to $4.9B. Source

Sources and gaps

FEMA figures come from OpenFEMA's Public Assistance and housing assistance datasets for disaster DR-4399-FL. Amounts are the federal share FEMA has obligated (committed), which can still change as projects close out. HUD awards come from the Governor's announcements; state money from a news summary of the 2019 budget; SBA and flood insurance from FEMA's January 2019 Bay County fact sheet.

What we couldn't find (9)
  • OpenFEMA PA data has no street address or coordinates for projects, only county and applicant. Location is limited to applicant and project title.
  • Applicant names come from the PublicAssistanceApplicants dataset. Bay District Schools appears as 'School Board of Bay County Florida'. Bay County utilities appear inside city or county applicants, not as separate utilities.
  • FEMA Hazard Mitigation Grant Program (HMGP) project-level awards for Bay County were not pulled. The disaster-wide HMGP total is included only from FemaWebDisasterSummaries.
  • CDBG-DR: no full DEO/FloridaCommerce list of every Michael subrecipient and amount was retrieved. Housing program (Rebuild Florida Hurricane Michael Housing Repair) spending in Bay County, later infrastructure rounds, and current drawdown or expenditure figures from HUD DRGR quarterly reports are missing. Hometown Revitalization amounts are rounded as published.
  • SBA loan, NFIP and early IHP figures for Bay are a January 2019 FEMA snapshot. Final SBA totals by county were not found.
  • Debris contracts: Bay County's AshBritt contract value, and the debris contractors and contract amounts for Lynn Haven, Callaway, Springfield, Parker and Mexico Beach, were not found in primary records. FEMA Category A obligations per government are included as a proxy for debris spending that was reimbursed.
  • 2019 state appropriations come from a news summary, not the General Appropriations Act line items. The $115M housing and $25M grant pool are regional, not Bay-only.
  • Lynn Haven case: court docket numbers and original judgment documents (PACER) were not pulled. Barnes's exact sentencing date and David White's sentence are from news reports. One Feb 2024 article calls a defendant 'David Mickey', which appears to be ECS owner David 'Mickey' White, and the May 2023 report names him David White.
  • OpenFEMA data was refreshed through 2026; obligations can still change as projects close out.
