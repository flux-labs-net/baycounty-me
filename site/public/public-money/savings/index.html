Records that would find more

The CRA ledger covers projects and grants, not every payment. These records, asked of each city and the county, would show subscriptions, duplicate payments and price differences.

  • Accounts payable check register or vendor payment history for the last three fiscal years, as a CSV or spreadsheet export
    Finds duplicate payments, the same vendor at different prices, and charges that grew with no contract change. Request this record
  • A list of software, license, subscription and telecom contracts, with vendor, annual cost and renewal date
    Finds overlapping subscriptions across departments and between cities. Request this record
  • Year-end CRA fund balances, with the project each balance is committed to
    Shows how much tax increment is sitting idle. Request this record

How this was built

  • Every figure comes from records already on the site: project histories on the Accountability console and rows of the CRA ledger. Each opportunity links the documents behind it.
  • An estimate multiplies the spending in play by a low and a high rate. The rate is the stated assumption, not a measured result. Where the records can't support a rate, the opportunity says which records are needed instead.
  • Totals add each opportunity once, but some overlap (engineering fees are part of project cost). Grants are included in project costs, so totals are more than local tax money alone.
  • Download everything as JSON, or use the open data and API. Corrections: send one.
