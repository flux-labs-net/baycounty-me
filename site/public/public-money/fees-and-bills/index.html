Taxes vs. fees, the simple version
  1. Property tax depends on your home's value. A bigger taxable value means a bigger tax.
  2. Fees on the tax bill are mostly flat. Non-ad valorem assessments charge per home, per parcel or by building size for a specific service, like fire protection or stormwater drainage. They're not affected by homestead exemptions or Amendment 3.
  3. Utility bills are separate. Water, sewer and garbage are billed monthly by your city or the county, based on how much you use.
