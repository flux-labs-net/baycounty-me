The simple version

How to read this

  1. The line on each bar is today's date in the fiscal year. Halfway through the year, a department at about half its budget is on pace.
  2. Committed money counts. An open purchase order is money promised to a vendor, so it's added to what's spent.
  3. Running ahead isn't always a problem. Insurance, software licenses and debt payments are often paid once a year, early. Going over the budget needs the commission to approve a budget amendment.

Where this comes from

  • The government's own budget-vs-actual report from its accounting system, as of each month end. A report is published only after its lines add up to the total printed on it.
  • The budget is the amended budget where the report gives one: the adopted budget plus any changes the commission approved during the year.
  • Departments are grouped as the report lists them. Revenue lines are shown separately and aren't counted as spending.
  • Data: /api/v1/budget-actual · every line: budget-actuals.
