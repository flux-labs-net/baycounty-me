Who gets paid: tourism agencies

Every payment by the three agencies that spend Bay County's tourist development tax on marketing, from the monthly check registers kept by the Clerk of Court. The county's own payments are on the Contracts page.

Registers through July 2026
County tourism agencies, by the city each one markets
The simple version

Where the bed tax goes after the county

  1. Visitors pay a 5% bed tax on hotel and rental stays. Part of it pays three agencies that market each tourism area: Panama City Beach, Panama City and Mexico Beach.
  2. The Clerk pays their bills. Each agency's payments are listed in the Clerk's monthly check register. That's what this page shows: $60.7M in payments over the months covered. A few months were never posted; they're listed under Sources and gaps.
  3. Some bills hide the details. Credit card payments and payroll services show as one payment; what was bought isn't listed. Payments to individual people are counted but not named.
In January 2026 the Clerk of Court published an action plan for Visit Panama City Beach, describing a criminal investigation and forensic audit and putting the agency's payments under the Clerk's review. The payments here are listed as recorded; this site makes no finding about any of them.

Notes on this register (4)
  • Source: the Bay County Clerk of Court & Comptroller's monthly 'Check Register - Sorted by Dollar Amount'. Each register has a section for the Panama City Beach Convention & Visitors Bureau and the Panama City and Mexico Beach Community Development Councils, the three agencies funded by the county's tourist development tax. As of Oct 21, 2026 all months come from the text-PDF copies in the Clerk's OnBase public records search ('BOCC - Check Registry Search', bayforms.baycoclerk.com), so no OCR was needed, except December 2024, which is missing from OnBase and was read by OCR from the scanned copy attached to the Feb 4, 2025 BOCC agenda. Every month's parsed rows were added up and match the printed section total to the cent (see reconciliation); no amounts were corrected. Vendor names are as printed, with spelling variants merged. Payments to individual people (performers, contractors, refunds, staff reimbursements, employee expense reports) are excluded from the vendor list and only counted in excluded_individuals. Payroll processors, withholding/benefit remittances, credit-card bill payments and unnamed bank transfers are kept but labeled in 'category'. Voided checks appear as negative amounts and are netted. Fiscal year = Oct 1-Sep 30.
  • Visit Panama City Beach is the Panama City Beach CVB, Inc., which runs marketing for the Bay County Tourist Development Council. Its money comes mainly from county transfers of tourist development tax (the BOCC register shows payments to the CVB of $11.5M, $18.2M and $9.1M in the FY2024-FY2026 months covered, per the Contracts page); this list shows how the CVB spent it.
  • November 2024: the register's CVB and Panama City CDC headings (and their printed totals) are swapped. Check-number series (CVB 29xxx-30xxx, CDC 10xxx) and vendors show which is which, and the rows here are assigned by content.
  • In January 2026 the Clerk published an 'Action Plan for Panama City Beach Convention & Visitors Bureau' after what it calls a discovery of flagrant fraud. It describes a criminal investigation and forensic audit as ongoing and moves CVB payments under Clerk pre-audit (apps.baycoclerk.com). The payments listed here are the payments as recorded; this file makes no finding about any of them.

Sources and gaps

The Bay County Clerk of Court's monthly "Check Register – Sorted by Dollar Amount," attached to County Commission agendas. Most months come from the Clerk's public records search as text PDFs; one month (December 2024) was only available as a scan and was read by OCR. Every month was added up and checked against the register's printed totals for each agency.

No city or the School Board posts a check register. You can request one; the request generator has them ready.

What we couldn't find (8)
  • City of Panama City: no check register, warrant list or vendor-payment report was found. Commission agenda packets (checked: Sep 8, 2026, Oct 13, 2026 and searched FY2023-FY2026 titles) have no such attachment. The city's Archive Center (budgets, ACFRs, pension reports) and site search had none. Needs a public-records request.
  • City of Panama City Beach: no register found. Council agendas on pcbgov.granicus.com (recent meetings checked) have no bills/warrant item, and pcbfl.gov blocks automated access (Cloudflare), so its document center could not be searched directly. There is no OpenGov/ClearGov transparency portal.
  • Callaway, Parker, Springfield, Mexico Beach: no registers. Callaway's recent agenda packets (CivicPlus) have none. Parker's Budget-Audits page holds only budgets and audits. Springfield's Finance page holds only budget resolutions and the 2025 audit. Mexico Beach's Budget & Financials page lists only budget/JLAC documents. The council minutes the site already has (data/contracts) do not itemize bills.
  • Bay District Schools: no warrant list or check register is posted. The Finance page has only budgets and fiscal-transparency graphs. Board agenda packets include 'Purchasing and Contracting' reports (purchase orders, not payments). bay.k12.fl.us uses a JavaScript challenge that blocks scripted downloads.
  • Visit Panama City Beach / Bay County TDC board packets (assets.simpleviewinc.com, e.g., the Sep 9, 2025 and Aug 26, 2025 agendas) have no check register, only individual invoice approvals (e.g., beach-nourishment engineering invoices in 2021 packets). The CVB's own payments are covered here through the Clerk's registers instead.
  • TDC agency months still missing: December 2025, January and February 2026 (not in the Clerk's OnBase register list; the BOCC agenda copy covering those months lists BOCC disbursements only, with CVB funding shown as BOCC transfers), and August-September 2026 (not posted yet). The OnBase document links are session-specific; the stable entry point is the Clerk's public records search, 'BOCC - Check Registry Search'.
  • Individuals were picked out by a first-name heuristic plus 'Last, First' patterns. A few sole proprietors may be misclassified either way; check excluded totals before quoting them.
  • Bay County BOCC FY2024-FY2026 and Lynn Haven FY2023-FY2025 registers are already summarized in the Contracts page (registers) and are not repeated here.
