What's in a report

  • The propertyUse, size, year built, values and sales since 2016
  • Tax estimateEvery taxing authority, the millage and flat fees
  • Flood and stormsFEMA flood zone, panel, evacuation zone
  • SchoolsElementary, middle and high school zones
  • Utilities and trash dayProviders, pickup days and rates
  • Zoning and developmentZoning, land use and projects nearby
  • Roads and projectsSales tax and road work within a mile
  • Parks and beachesThe closest parks and beach accesses
  • Who represents itCommission, city, school board, state and U.S.

Reports come from public records: the county's map server, FEMA, the Florida Department of Revenue and the datasets on this site. Owner names and private details are never included. Your branding and report history are kept private to your account; you can delete them any time.
