The simple version

Why the bases matter here

  1. Two bases, thousands of jobs. Tyndall flies F-35A fighters and runs air defense for the continental U.S. NSA Panama City is home to a Navy research lab and the dive school for every branch.
  2. Federal money reaches local companies. Shipbuilders, engineers and contractors based in Bay County win contracts from the Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard and other agencies. That money is listed below by company.
  3. Rebuilding after Michael. Hurricane Michael wrecked Tyndall in 2018. Its rebuild is one of the largest construction projects in the region.

The two bases

Tyndall Air Force Base

Air Force · 325th Fighter Wing (Air Combat Command)

Home of the 325th Fighter Wing, which is becoming an operational F-35A Lightning II wing (the first F-35As arrived August 1, 2023). Also home to First Air Force and its 601st Air and Space Operations Center, which run air defense of the continental U.S., the 53rd Weapons Evaluation Group and the Air Force Civil Engineer Center.

Hurricane Michael hit the base on October 10, 2018. In April 2019 the Air Force estimated the damage at $4.7 billion; the base is being rebuilt as an "installation of the future."

In command: Col. Ryan Thulin, 325th Fighter Wing commander, since Jun 23, 2026.

Naval Support Activity Panama City

Navy · Naval Support Activity Panama City (Navy Region Southeast)

Runs the base for its tenant commands. The largest, Naval Surface Warfare Center Panama City Division, is one of the Navy's research and test labs for mine warfare, diving and coastal operations. The Naval Diving and Salvage Training Center trains divers from every service.

In command: Cmdr. Tristan V. Oliveria, Commanding officer, since Feb 12, 2026.

Where this comes from

  • News is read every day from DVIDS, where the public affairs offices of the 325th Fighter Wing, the 601st Air and Space Operations Center, NSA Panama City, Naval Surface Warfare Center Panama City Division and the Navy Experimental Diving Unit post their stories. The base websites turn automated readers away. Each feed shows only its latest stories, so older stories fill in over time.
  • Base facts and commanders are checked by hand against the units' own stories and public base histories. Commanders change every two to three years.
  • Federal contracts come from USAspending.gov: every contract action since January 1, 2016 for a company whose registered business address is in Bay County. Amounts are money obligated (committed), not paid out, and include modifications and cuts. Street addresses are not shown.
  • Company links: a contractor is joined to a local vendor or a business campaign donor when the names match; otherwise it gets its own profile.
  • Data: military-installations (CSV) · military-news (CSV) · federal-contracts (CSV) · federal-contract-years (CSV).
