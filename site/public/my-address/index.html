    How this works

    Addresses, parcels, property values and district boundaries come live from Bay County's public GIS server, which publishes the Property Appraiser's parcel data. Property values are the 2026 roll. Flood zones come live from FEMA's National Flood Hazard Layer, the official current flood map, with the FIRM panel and any map revision (LOMR) that applies.

    The tax estimate applies the latest complete set of official rates (tax year 2025, FY2026 bills, from the Florida Department of Revenue) to the 2026 values. The rates adopted in September 2026 will replace them when the state publishes the full set. Flat non-ad valorem fees (fire, stormwater, lighting and similar) come from the Florida Department of Revenue's parcel-level file for the 2025 tax roll, so they show what was on the FY2026 bill. For a homestead, school tax is figured on the value before the second homestead exemption, since that one doesn't apply to schools. Other exemptions are taken as reported by the Property Appraiser. Your official bill comes from the Tax Collector.
